# Multi-Contour Fusion Evaluation
## Edge-case (30 images) — Fusion ON vs OFF

### Without Fusion (Task 2 baseline):
- Exact accuracy: 4/30 = 13.3%
- Close accuracy: 5/30 = 16.7%
- Contour found: 29/30 = 96.7%
- Empty↔full flips: 4 (empty→high: 3, high→empty: 1)
- ACCR-02 target: NOT MET (4 > 3)

### With Fusion (top-3 contours, weighted avg):
- Exact accuracy: 6/30 = 20.0% (+6.7pp improvement)
- Close accuracy: 7/30 = 23.3% (+6.6pp)
- Contour found: 29/30 = 96.7% (same)
- Empty↔full flips: 6 (empty→high: 3, high→empty: 3)
- ACCR-02 target: NOT MET (6 > 3)

### Trade-off Assessment:
+ Exact accuracy improved by 6.7pp (20% vs 13.3%)
- Empty↔full confusion worsened (6 vs 4)
- Primary ACCR-02 metric worsened
+ Low-fill bucket accuracy improved (50% vs 0% for very_low)
- High→empty flips increased (3 vs 1)

### Decision: REVERT — Fusion DISABLED
Fusion does not improve the primary ACCR-02 target (≤3 empty↔full flips).
The trade-off (better exact accuracy, worse confusion) does not meet the
project's primary accuracy requirement. Single best-contour retained.
